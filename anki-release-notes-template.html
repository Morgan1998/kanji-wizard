About this Deck

Hi! I'm Morgan. I'm an aspiring software engineer living in Osaka, Japan. For the last year and a half I've been studying web development primarily via The Odin Project. Last year, I took the JLPT N3 in December. I passed! Woohoo! My strongest area was without a doubt kanji. I developed a solid system that allowed me to fly through the first section of the test. Some months later after the test I got to thinking: since I'm studying software engineering, why not try to make an app that includes all the tools and conveniences that I wish I had during those grueling study sessions? And so the idea of making Kanji Wizard was born. I named it Kanji "Wizard" because I like to jokingly call myself or my friends a wizard when we do something cool xD. Anyway, take a look below to see what you can do with Kanji Wizard:

The Kanji Stuff

Radical Highlighting & Stroke Numbers

Drawing Practice Board

Stroke Advancer Sigil. Just tap the sigil to advance the strokes. :D

Readings & Meanings

Reading Filters (On / Kun / Irregular)

Alternative Meanings Drawer. You can view all meanings and optionally swap out your hook meaning. (The meaning that best helps you remember the kanji.)

Vocabulary Ledger & Tools

Selection Mode (🔮) with dictionary lookup or copy to clipboard. You can change your dictionary in the settings. There are 4 options :P

Progressive Vocab Reveal Sigil. Put the sigil wherever you want and tap it to advance your vocab reveals.
