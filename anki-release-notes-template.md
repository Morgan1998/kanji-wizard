## About the `Kanji Wizard` Decks

Hi! I'm Morgan. I'm an aspiring software engineer living in Osaka, Japan. For the last year and a half I've been studying web development primarily via The Odin Project. Last year, I took the JLPT N3 in December. I passed! Woohoo! My strongest area was without a doubt kanji. I developed a solid system that allowed me to fly through the first section of the test. Some months later after the test I got to thinking: since I'm studying software engineering, why not try to make an app that includes all the tools and conveniences that I wish I had during those grueling study sessions? And so the idea of making Kanji Wizard was born. I named it Kanji "Wizard" because I like to jokingly call myself or my friends a wizard when we do something cool xD. Anyway, take a look below to see what you can do with Kanji Wizard!

### Meanings & Readings

**Alternative Meanings Drawer** You can view all meanings and optionally swap out your hook meaning by tapping one of the meanings in the drawer. (I call the meaning that best helps me remember the kanji the _hook meaning_.)

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f885d803-376f-4b76-b635-04cc974ed1b4" width="320" alt="Alternative Meanings Drawer Feature" />

<br><br>

**Reading Filters (On / Kun / Irregular)**
This is off by default because Japanese irregular readings have many complex edge cases. Jukujikun (phrase-based readings), Rendaku (voicing changes), and specialized phonetic shifts heavily impact how words are read. Because I have to manually verify every multi-kanji word to ensure accuracy (I'm not done, sorry!), please take this feature with a grain of salt for now! Or just keep this feature off. Note: even with it off, you can still use the On and Kun buttons!

![Reading Filters Demo](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8b6a729f-e3cb-4c54-ab6d-b9a655be1327)

<br><br>

### Vocabulary Ledger & Tools

**Selection Mode (🔮)!** You can select all kanji of a word or just the kanji you don't know in a word to look them up instantly in a dictionary (tapping 'search' takes your selected kanji(s), opens a new tab in your device's browser, and makes a query to your selected dictionary). I really wish I had this when I was studying because I constantly would be manually copying kanji I didn't know from a word, switching over to my dictionary, and pasting it into the search bar. Oh, and you can change your dictionary in the settings! There are 4 options :P

![Selection Mode Demo](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/52b97cb0-4fcc-454d-907a-de3816f95683)

<br><br>

**Vocab Reveal Sigil** Put the sigil wherever you want and tap it to sequentially reveal the furigana and meanings of your vocab. You can alternatively directly tap the vocab to reveal its furigana or meaning.
