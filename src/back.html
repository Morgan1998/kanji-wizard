Sort Priorities Info
🗣️ Spoken Frequency

Based on the CEJC (Corpus of Everyday Japanese Conversion). It orders words based on spoken corpus frequency (conversations, everyday speech). The creators interviewed people over a span of four years from 2016 to 2020. Still considered a great standard for spoken Japanese word frequency ratings

📺 YouTube Occurrences

Based on the TUBELEX project. It orders words based on occurrences in YouTube subtitles. This is a newer project that seems very promising

📚 Literary Frequency

Based on the JPDB frequency list. It orders words based on written corpus frequency (novels, newspapers, Wikipedia). Usually not the best option for seeing commonly used words in daily spoken Japanese

Search Dictionaries Info
⛩️ Jisho.org

The standard Japanese-English dictionary. Best for clear translations, sentence examples, and radical breakdowns.

🔮 Mazii

A multi-language Japanese dictionary. Best for learners seeking definitions in English, French, Vietnamese, and other languages.

📚 Weblio

A highly detailed Japanese-English and Japanese-to-Japanese dictionary. Best for intermediate and advanced sentence contextualization.

🦊 Kotobank

A comprehensive monolingual Japanese-to-Japanese dictionary. Best for advanced learners studying native dictionary definitions.
